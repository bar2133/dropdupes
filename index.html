KeepBest

Photo Cleaner for iPhone

KeepBest finds duplicate and near-identical photos in your library, groups them, and suggests the best shot of each group, so you can free up space in a few taps.

  • Duplicates: similar shots grouped together, with the sharpest, best-framed photo suggested as the one to keep.
  • Large Files: your biggest photos and videos, largest first.
  • Look closely: long press any photo to see it whole and zoom in.
  • Safe: nothing is deleted until you confirm, iOS asks again, and deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Private: photos are analyzed on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, there's no account, and there's no tracking.

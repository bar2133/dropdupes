Questions

Does Burstwise upload my photos?

No. Everything runs on your iPhone. Burstwise makes no network requests of its own and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.

Will it delete anything without asking?

No. You see a summary first, then iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted items go to Photos › Recently Deleted for 30 days.

How does it pick the best shot?

It prefers favorites, then photos without letterboxing, then the sharper, higher-resolution one. It's only a suggestion (★): you decide what to keep.

What do I get with Pro?

A one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited cleanups, Keep Best in every group at once, and Large Files. Restore it on any iPhone with the same Apple Account.

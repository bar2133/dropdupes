In short: Burstwise works entirely on your iPhone. Your photos, and everything the app learns about them, never leave your device. We don't collect, share or sell any data, there's no account, and there's no tracking or advertising.

What the app accesses

With your permission, Burstwise reads your Photo Library to find duplicates and large files. You can give access to all photos or only selected ones, and change this at any time in Settings → Burstwise → Photos.

How your photos are processed

Photos are analyzed on your device with Apple's Vision framework. The app turns each photo into a numeric "fingerprint" to compare it with others. Your photos and these fingerprints are never sent to us or anyone else. If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple so it can be analyzed. That transfer is between your device and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

What is stored, and where

To make later scans faster and remember your choices, Burstwise stores the following only in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • photo fingerprints and basic photo details (date, size, location flag, whether it's a screenshot or favorite);
  • your review progress (which photos you marked to keep or delete);
  • a history of cleanups (how many items and how much space);
  • your settings, and timing and memory statistics about scans, which never leave the device.

This data is included in your device backups like any app data. Deleting the app removes it.

What we collect

Nothing. Burstwise has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party code that collects data, and it doesn't connect to any server of ours. In the App Store's terms: Data Not Collected.

Purchases

Burstwise Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details or personal information. The app checks your purchase on the device. It keeps a count of your free deletions (a single number) in your keychain; if iCloud Keychain is on, Apple syncs that number end-to-end encrypted to your other devices, so the count follows your Apple Account. We never receive it.

Deleting photos

Burstwise deletes photos only when you confirm, through the standard iOS prompt. Deleted items go to Photos → Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.

Children

Burstwise doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If the app ever starts handling data differently, we'll say so here before that version is released.

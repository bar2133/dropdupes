In short: Your photos stay on your phone. We don't collect, share or sell any data, there's no account, and there's no tracking or advertising.

What the app accesses

With your permission, DropDupes reads your Photo Library to find duplicates and large files, and to sort photos for Clean Up. You can give access to all photos or only selected ones, and change this at any time in Settings → DropDupes → Photos.

How your photos are processed

Your photos stay on your phone. They are analyzed with Apple's Vision framework, and the app turns each photo into a numeric "fingerprint" to compare it with others. If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple so it can be analyzed. That transfer is between your device and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

What is stored, and where

To make later scans faster and remember your choices, DropDupes keeps information about your photos and your choices only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage.

Your review progress, cleanup history and settings are included in your device backups like any app data. Deleting the app removes everything in its own storage.

What we collect

Nothing. DropDupes has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party code that collects data, and it doesn't connect to any server of ours. In the App Store's terms: Data Not Collected.

Purchases

DropDupes Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details or personal information. The app checks your purchase on the device. It keeps a count of your free deletions (a single number) in your keychain; if iCloud Keychain is on, Apple syncs that number end-to-end encrypted to your other devices, so the count follows your Apple Account. We never receive it.

Deleting photos

DropDupes deletes photos only when you confirm, through the standard iOS prompt. Deleted items go to Photos → Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.

Children

DropDupes doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If the app ever starts handling data differently, we'll say so here before that version is released.

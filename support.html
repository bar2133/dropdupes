Purchases

What is free, and what does DropDupes Pro add?

Scanning, reviewing and your first 20 deletions are free. DropDupes Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks unlimited cleanups, Keep Best in every group at once, Large Files, and combined filters in Clean Up.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Open Settings › DropDupes Pro in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

Photo access

Why does DropDupes need access to my photos?

To find duplicates and sort photos for Clean Up, it has to look at them. Your photos stay on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

I chose "Limited Access". Why are some duplicates missing?

DropDupes can only see the photos you selected. To scan everything, go to Settings → DropDupes → Photos and choose Full Access.

I turned access off. How do I turn it back on?

Settings → DropDupes → Photos → Full Access.

Deleting and recovering

Will DropDupes delete anything without asking?

No. Nothing is marked for deletion until you choose. Before anything is deleted, DropDupes shows a summary, then iOS asks you to confirm.

I deleted the wrong photo. Can I get it back?

Yes. Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted and tap Recover. Items stay there for 30 days.

My storage didn't go up after deleting.

Deleted items still take space while they're in Recently Deleted. Empty that album in Photos to free the space right away.

Scanning and best shots

What does the similarity slider do?

It sets how alike photos must be to be grouped. Higher means only near-identical shots; lower also groups photos that are merely similar. Changing it regroups instantly without rescanning.

How does DropDupes choose the best shot?

It prefers favorites, then photos that aren't letterboxed, then the sharper one, the higher resolution, the larger file, and finally the oldest. It's only a suggestion (★); you decide what to keep.

The first scan is slow.

The first scan analyzes every photo; later scans only look at new or edited ones. Keep DropDupes open while it scans. Photos stored only in iCloud may need to download first.

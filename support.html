Frequently asked questions

What is free, and what does Burstwise Pro add?

Scanning, reviewing and your first 20 deletions are free. Burstwise Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks unlimited cleanups, Keep Best in every group at once, and Large Files.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Open Settings › Burstwise Pro in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

Why does Burstwise need access to my photos?

To find duplicates it has to look at your photos. It does this on your iPhone; nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

I chose "Limited Access". Why are some duplicates missing?

Burstwise can only see the photos you selected. To scan everything, go to Settings → Burstwise → Photos and choose Full Access.

I turned access off. How do I turn it back on?

Settings → Burstwise → Photos → Full Access.

Will Burstwise delete anything without asking?

No. Nothing is marked for deletion until you choose. Before anything is deleted, Burstwise shows a summary, then iOS asks you to confirm.

I deleted the wrong photo. Can I get it back?

Yes. Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted and tap Recover. Items stay there for 30 days.

My storage didn't go up after deleting.

Deleted items still take space while they're in Recently Deleted. Empty that album in Photos to free the space right away.

What does the similarity slider do?

It sets how alike photos must be to be grouped. Higher means only near-identical shots; lower also groups photos that are merely similar. Changing it regroups instantly without rescanning.

How does Burstwise choose the best shot?

It prefers favorites, then photos that aren't letterboxed, then the sharper one, the higher resolution, the larger file, and finally the oldest. It's only a suggestion (★); you decide what to keep.

The first scan is slow.

The first scan analyzes every photo; later scans only look at new or edited ones. Keep Burstwise open while it scans. Photos stored only in iCloud may need to download first.
